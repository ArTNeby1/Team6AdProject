3 Days in Singapore - My Full Itinerary

Day 1 (Aug 12): Landed in the morning and dropped bags at the hotel near Bugis. First stop was Gardens by the Bay - walked through the Cloud Forest dome and the Flower Dome (tickets were about SGD 32 combined), then waited around for the Super Tree Grove light show at 7:45pm, which was free and honestly the highlight of the day. Grabbed dinner right after at Satay by the Bay, a hawker-style outdoor court next to the gardens.

Day 2 (Aug 13): Spent the whole morning at the Merlion Park taking photos, it's a quick 15-minute stop but great for pictures with Marina Bay Sands in the background. Walked over to Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck in the afternoon (SGD 26 entry), amazing 360 view of the city. In the evening we did the Chinatown Street Market on Pagoda Street - lots of souvenir shopping and tried the famous Chinatown Complex Food Centre for cheap Michelin-recommended chicken rice.

Day 3 (Aug 14): Took the MRT to Sentosa Island in the morning for Universal Studios Singapore (full day, tickets SGD 78), then in the evening watched the Wings of Time show on the beach for free. Flight back was at 11pm so we grabbed a quick meal at Vivo City mall food court beforehand.

Overall a packed but doable 3-day trip, would recommend booking Gardens by the Bay tickets online in advance to skip the queue.
