Quick Singapore Food Trip

Didn't plan much, just went to Singapore for a long weekend to eat. Started at Tekka Centre in Little India in the evening for briyani and roti prata, super cheap, no fixed hours but busiest after 6pm. Next day walked around the Haji Lane area to see the colourful shophouses and street art murals (free, just wander around), then had lunch at Zam Zam Restaurant, a nearly 100-year-old stall near Sultan Mosque that's been around forever. In the afternoon checked out Buddha Tooth Relic Temple in Chinatown, entry is free but the relic viewing on the upper floor asks for a small donation, took about 2 hours round trip including travel. Didn't note down exact addresses for most of these since we just used Google Maps on the spot, but they're all easy to find by name.
