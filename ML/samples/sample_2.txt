Singapore Weekend Guide (2 Days)

Friday: Arrived around noon, checked into a hotel near Clarke Quay. Headed straight to Thian Hock Keng Temple in Chinatown - entry is free, best visited before sunset for photos, we got there around 5pm. Walked a few minutes over to Sri Mariamman Temple to see the colourful gopuram, only takes about 20 minutes. For dinner we went to Maxwell Food Centre, home to the famous Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice stall, no reservations, expect to queue 15-20 minutes.

Saturday: Morning trip to Tiong Bahru Market - it's a local wet market and hawker centre, budget about 2 hours, mostly local breakfast food and produce stalls, entry is free. In the afternoon we relaxed with a foot massage near the hotel (around SGD 45/hour). Ended the trip with rooftop drinks at 1-Altitude for the skyline view before our late flight home.

Tip: Thian Hock Keng and Sri Mariamman are within walking distance of each other so pair them on the same day.
